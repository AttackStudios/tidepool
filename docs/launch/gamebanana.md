# GameBanana — game and tool submission

The fastest of the real mod sites to accept a brand-new game, so this is where you plant a flag while
Thunderstore and Nexus requests sit in a queue.

## 1. Request the game

Submit Surf Sandbox as a new game.

- **Name:** Surf Sandbox
- **Homepage:** https://store.steampowered.com/app/4480760/Surf_Sandbox/
- **Developer:** nocanwin
- **Release:** 25 August 2026
- **Why:** Unity title running BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP), with an open-source mod manager and an in-game
  break editor — "name your spot, restore the reef" — that makes shareable custom breaks an obvious
  content category.

## 2. Submit TidePool as a Tool

**Name:** TidePool Mod Manager

**Summary:** Free, open-source mod manager for Surf Sandbox — browse, install, update and launch.

**Description:**

> TidePool installs mods for Surf Sandbox and gets out of the way.
>
> - **Browse and install** from Thunderstore, with dependencies resolved and installed in the right
>   order automatically. The detail panel shows exactly what will land before you commit.
> - **Profiles** keep separate sets of mods. Switching never touches your game install, so vanilla is
>   always one click away.
> - **One-click launch** straight from the app, or hand off to Steam if you want the overlay and
>   playtime. A Vanilla button starts the game unmodded, which is the fastest way to tell whether a
>   mod caused a bug.
> - **Enable or disable** mods without uninstalling, and update them individually or all at once.
> - **Works offline** — the package list is cached, so a dropped connection shows a stale list rather
>   than an error page.
>
> Windows installer and portable build. macOS build available for development. MIT licensed, source at
> https://github.com/AttackStudios/tidepool.
