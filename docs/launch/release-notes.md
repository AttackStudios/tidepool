[quiet]

Free, open-source mod manager for **Surf Sandbox**.

### New in 1.0.4

- **Install from file.** A mod someone sent you as a zip now installs with a button, instead of
  "unzip this into your game folder, keeping the folder names" — which is exactly how a DLL ends
  up in the wrong place and a working mod gets reported as broken. An already-extracted folder
  works too, since plenty of people unzip before asking what to do.

### Everything else

Browse and install mods with dependencies resolved, keep separate profiles, manage and share
beaches, and launch straight from the app.

- **Beach Manager.** Reads the levels in your game install, shows what each one actually is —
  maximum depth in metres, swell, and whether the sea floor bottoms out on the game's limit — and
  installs, shares and deletes them. The folder is resolved from the game every time, so it
  follows a moved or reinstalled copy without being told.
- **Break Pack.** Nine real waves rebuilt from their actual sea-floor shape.
- **Profiles.** Separate setups, swapped instantly, with vanilla always one click away.
- **Sharing.** A profile or a beach travels as a single code.
