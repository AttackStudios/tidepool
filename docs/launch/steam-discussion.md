**Title:** TidePool — free, open-source mod manager for Surf Sandbox

---

Surf Sandbox doesn't have official mod support, but it's a Unity game, so the community can add it.
I've been building a mod manager for it ahead of release, and it's out today alongside the game.

**TidePool** installs mods and gets out of the way:

- Browse and install mods in one click — dependencies are resolved and installed in the right order
- Separate profiles, so you can keep a clean setup and a modded one and switch between them
- Switching profiles never touches your game install, so vanilla is always one click away
- Share a whole setup, or a custom break, as a code — no account, no server
- Launch straight from the app, or hand off to Steam so you keep the overlay and playtime

Free, open source, MIT licensed, no account: {{RELEASE_URL}}

Step-by-step setup guide: {{STEAM_GUIDE_URL}}

**Two things worth saying plainly.**

This is unofficial and not affiliated with nocanwin. Mods are not supported by the developer.

If you're running mods and something breaks, **please don't report it to nocanwin**. Surf Sandbox is
made by one person, and modded bug reports cost them time on problems they didn't cause. Launch
vanilla first to check, and if a mod is the cause bring it to us instead — Discord at
https://discord.gg/RRu3gevbXS or GitHub issues.

Happy to answer anything in the thread.
