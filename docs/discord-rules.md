# 🌊 TidePool Rules

**1 · Be decent.** No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
**2 · No piracy.** Never request or share cracked copies, game files, or decompiled source. Own the game.
**3 · Respect nocanwin.** Don't pressure the dev about mods, and never report a modded bug as vanilla.
**4 · Safe mods only.** Official sources. Hidden or obfuscated code means a permanent ban.
**5 · Credit others.** Don't reupload or repackage someone's mod as your own.
**6 · Ask in #help.** Include your `BepInEx/LogOutput.log` and mod list. No DMs for help.
**7 · No spam or ads.** No mass pings or unrelated promo.
**8 · Keep it SFW.** Chat, profiles, and nicknames.
**9 · Discord's [Terms](https://discord.com/terms) apply.** 13+ only.
**10 · Staff have the final say.** No list covers everything.
