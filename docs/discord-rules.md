# 🌊 WaterWay — Server Rules

This is the community building mod tools for **Surf Sandbox**. Read these before posting. Staying here means you accept them.

**1 · Be decent**
No harassment, hate speech, slurs, or personal attacks. Argue with the idea, never the person.

**2 · No piracy**
Do not request, link, or share cracked copies of Surf Sandbox, game files, assets, or decompiled source. Own the game. This one is non-negotiable — it is what keeps this server and our platform listings alive.

**3 · Respect nocanwin**
Surf Sandbox is made by one person. Don't pressure or pile on the developer about mod support, and never report a modded bug to them as if it were vanilla. If a mod broke it, it's ours to fix.

**4 · Only share safe mods**
Link mods from their official source. Never distribute compiled DLLs with hidden or obfuscated behaviour. Malware means an immediate permanent ban and a report.

**5 · Credit and permissions**
Don't reupload, repackage, or republish someone else's mod as your own. Ask first, credit always.

**6 · Use the right channel**
Keep support requests in #support, and include your `BepInEx/LogOutput.log` and your mod list. Don't DM staff or mod authors for help.

**7 · No spam or unsolicited advertising**
No mass pings, chain messages, or promoting unrelated servers and projects.

**8 · Keep it clean**
No NSFW, gore, or shock content anywhere, including profiles and nicknames.

**9 · Follow Discord's rules**
Discord's [Terms](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines) apply here. You must be 13 or older.

**10 · Staff have the final say**
No list covers everything. Moderators may act on anything that harms the community, listed here or not.

-# Something wrong, or think a decision was a mistake? Open a ticket in #modmail rather than handling it in chat.
