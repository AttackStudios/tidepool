Browse and install

MANAGER

Search Thunderstore, pick a mod, press install. Dependencies are resolved and installed in the right order, and the panel shows exactly what will land before you commit to anything.

  • Dependencies handled. Installing one mod brings everything it needs, in the correct order.
  • Three sources. Curated Essentials, all of Thunderstore, and GameBanana listings.
  • Updates. Per mod or all at once, and it never offers you a downgrade.
  • Works offline. The package list is cached, so a dropped connection shows a stale list rather than an error.

Keep your setups apart

PROFILES

Every profile is its own set of mods with its own BepInEx folder. Switching between them never touches your game install, so getting back to vanilla is always one click — and you can hand a whole setup to a friend as a code.

  • Your install stays clean. Mods live in the profile, not the game folder.
  • Share a setup as a code. No account, no server. A five-mod profile fits in about 230 characters.
  • Toggle without uninstalling. Turn a mod off, keep its settings.
  • Told before you break things. Removing a mod warns you what depends on it.

No Workshop? No problem

BEACHES

Surf Sandbox saves every beach you build as a plain file, and there is no Steam Workshop to trade them through. TidePool finds them, lists them, and turns any one into a code that carries the whole beach — so sending someone your break is one paste.

  • Needs no mod loader. Beaches are save files, so this works without BepInEx.
  • Nothing gets overwritten. An imported beach lands beside your own, never on top of it.
  • Finds the folder itself. Or point it at one, if your saves live somewhere unusual.

When something goes wrong

LOGS

Mods break. When they do, the Logs tab shows the loader output with errors picked out, and one button copies a support bundle — the log, your mod list, versions, the lot — so asking for help is a single paste instead of ten messages.

  • Errors first. Filter to just the failures instead of scrolling.
  • One-click support bundle. Everything a helper needs, already gathered.
  • Launch vanilla. The quickest way to prove whether a mod caused it.
